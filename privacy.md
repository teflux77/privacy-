# Privacy Notice

This is a personal, non-commercial software integration for the owner's own use.
It accesses, strictly in read-only mode (EU PSD2 Account Information Service),
the bank accounts of its sole user at their own bank.

- Data retrieved: balances and transactions of the owner's own accounts.
- Purpose: personal budget tracking only.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
- Bank credentials are handled by the bank and the licensed aggregator during
  authorization and are never stored by this application.
- Consent can be revoked at any time from the bank's online banking interface.
